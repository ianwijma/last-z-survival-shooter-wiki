# Summary

This community wiki page provides comprehensive information about the Shambler enemy type, including base statistics, behavior patterns, weaknesses, and variant types. The Shambler is described as the most common enemy in Last Z, first introduced in Season 1.

# Claims by topic

## enemies/shambler

- Health Points: 150
- Attack Damage: 15
- Attack Speed: 1.5 seconds
- Movement Speed: 2.5 m/s
- Detection Range: 15 meters
- Aggro Range: 25 meters
- Typically appears in groups of 3-8
- Found in all game modes and on all maps
- Headshots deal 2x damage
- Takes 50% increased damage from fire-based weapons and abilities
- Leg shots slow movement speed by 40% for 3 seconds
- Melee range: 1.5 meters
- First introduced in Season 1

## enemies/shambler/bloated

- Health Points: 300
- Explodes on death, dealing 40 damage in a 3-meter radius
- Identified by swollen appearance and greenish tint
- Described as a rare variant

## enemies/shambler/armored

- Health Points: 200
- Takes 30% reduced damage from body shots
- Headshots remain fully effective (no damage reduction)
- Introduced in Season 2
