# Shambler

The Shambler is a common infected-type enemy encountered throughout *Last Z: Survival Shooter*. First introduced in Season 1, Shamblers are the most frequently encountered enemy type in the game.

## Overview

Shamblers are slow-moving infected that typically appear in groups of 3-8. While individually weak, they can overwhelm unprepared players through sheer numbers. They are found in all game modes and on all maps.

## Statistics

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Health Points | 150 |
| Attack Damage | 15 |
| Attack Speed | 1.5 seconds |
| Movement Speed | 2.5 m/s |
| Detection Range | 15 meters |
| Aggro Range | 25 meters |

## Behavior

Shamblers exhibit predictable behavior patterns:

1. **Idle State**: When no player is detected, Shamblers wander slowly in random directions.
2. **Detection**: Upon detecting a player within 15 meters, the Shambler will turn and begin moving toward the target.
3. **Aggro**: Once a Shambler has aggroed, it will continue pursuing the player up to 25 meters before losing interest.
4. **Attack**: When within melee range (1.5 meters), the Shambler attacks every 1.5 seconds for 15 damage.

## Weaknesses

- **Headshots**: Deal 2x damage, making them highly effective against Shamblers.
- **Fire**: Shamblers take 50% increased damage from fire-based weapons and abilities.
- **Leg shots**: Shooting a Shambler's legs will slow its movement speed by 40% for 3 seconds.

## Variants

### Bloated Shambler

A rare variant with 300 HP that explodes on death, dealing 40 damage in a 3-meter radius. Identified by its swollen appearance and greenish tint.

### Armored Shambler

Introduced in Season 2, this variant has 200 HP and takes 30% reduced damage from body shots. Headshots remain fully effective.

## Tips

- Use melee weapons to conserve ammunition when dealing with small groups.
- Aim for headshots to maximize damage efficiency.
- Keep moving to avoid being surrounded.
- Fire-based weapons are particularly effective in later waves.

## Trivia

- Shamblers were the first enemy type revealed during the game's development.
- Their design was inspired by classic zombie media.
